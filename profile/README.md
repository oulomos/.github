Oulomos is a modelling tool for many kinds of modelling, such as enterprise
architecture and class-based or fact-based information modelling.

- **Models are plain text.** A model is a folder of text files: readable without
  the tool, diffable in Git, and easy to work on with an AI agent.
- **Notations are extensions.** Each notation brings its own templates and a
  description of how it is drawn. Adding one never touches the tool's code.
- **Extensible all the way down.** The layers beneath the notations, down to the
  core, can be extended or replaced, so Oulomos can grow into kinds of modelling
  it does not support yet.

## Status

🚧 Oulomos is in early, experimental development, and its repositories are still
private. We plan to open them under the [EUPL-1.2](https://eupl.eu/1.2/en/)
around the turn of the year, late 2026 or early 2027.

**Stay informed:** follow this organisation on GitHub, and the repositories will
appear here as soon as they go public.
